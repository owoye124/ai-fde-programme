# Day 1: What an AI FDE is

## In my own words
An AI Forward Deployed Engineer is a technical professional who works directly with organisations to solve real-world problems using AI. They understand the customer's problem, work with their data, build an AI solution, and integrate it into the organisation's existing systems. They also test, deploy, and improve the solution to make sure it works effectively in the real world. In simple terms, an AI FDE connects AI technology with real business problems and helps turn ideas into working solutions.

## The FDE loop

Understand -> Decide -> Build -> Deploy -> Measure -> Explain

## The one thing I want to remember
An AI FDE does not start with AI technology, they start by understanding the real problem and then use AI only when it is the right solution.
